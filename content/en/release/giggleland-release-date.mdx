## Streaming and Recording: The Unusual Selling Point

Plenty of horror games advertise a streamer mode that hides copyrighted music or blurs gore. Giggleland takes a different route: the developers state the full game is safe to stream and record with no special mode required. The only instruction is to capture the game window rather than your entire desktop.

That's a small detail with real consequences. Window capture keeps notifications, browser tabs, and anything else on your screen out of the broadcast, which matters more than usual here — the store page hints that some of the game's moments reach beyond the game itself.

| Action | Recommended? | Why |
