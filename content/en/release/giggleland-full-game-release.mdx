## Streaming Giggleland: What Creators Need to Know

The most interesting line on the store page is the streaming policy. Giggleland is explicitly safe to stream and record with no streamer mode required — a rare bit of good news for content creators who usually have to tiptoe around music licensing or spoiler-gated sections. The full game can be captured from start to finish.

There is one specific instruction attached, and it matters: capture the **game window** rather than your entire screen. The reason is baked into the game's design. The store page hints that some of Giggleland's effects occur outside the game itself, which is exactly the kind of trick that breaks if your capture software is recording your whole desktop. In practice, that means:

- Set your capture source to window capture, not display capture, in OBS or your recording software of choice.
- Close anything on your desktop you would not want appearing in a highlight reel.
- Test your scene setup before you go live on launch day, not during the first scare.
- Keep the game in windowed or borderless mode if your capture tool struggles to isolate the window.

Following that one instruction keeps you inside the developer's stated safe-to-stream policy and protects you from accidentally broadcasting whatever the game decides to do beyond its own borders.
